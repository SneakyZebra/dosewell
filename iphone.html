DoseWell for iPhone

Keep track of what you took and when your next dose is scheduled. Open this link in Safari to get started. No account or App Store download is required.

Open DoseWell

  1. Add DoseWell to your Home Screen

    Tap Open DoseWell above. In Safari, tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). Depending on your layout, you may need to open the page menu first. Select Add to Home Screen, turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

    Open the new DoseWell icon before entering medications. Use that icon each time so you return to the same saved plan. You can also use DoseWell entirely in Safari.

  2. Add your first medication

    On Today, tap Add medication. Type the details from your current label or scan the label and check what was captured. Enter your dose, choose a schedule, and tap Save medication. Choose As needed for a medicine you take only when needed.

  3. Set up and test Lock Screen reminders

    In the planner, open Reminders. Under Apple Calendar reminders, save or share your calendar file, then import its events into a personal calendar. A separate calendar called DoseWell makes future updates easier. The file contains medication names and doses.

    In iPhone Settings → Notifications → Calendar, allow notifications and select Lock Screen. Back in DoseWell, choose Try a test alert, import the sample event, then lock your phone. Expect one alert in about eight minutes and another in about ten minutes.

    Open reminder setup

  4. Record a dose and keep alerts current

    After you take a scheduled dose, open Today, tap Mark taken, and confirm the time. For an as needed medication, open Medications → Log a dose. Your entries appear in History.

    If a schedule changes or a medicine is stopped, remove its outdated events in Apple Calendar. Import the updated schedule for any reminders you still need. Logging or snoozing a dose in DoseWell leaves imported Calendar alerts in place.

I cannot find a setup option or hear the test alert

If Safari does not show Add to Home Screen, scroll to the bottom of its Share menu and tap Edit Actions. Apple’s Home Screen instructions

If Calendar is missing when you open the file, attach the saved .ics file to an email to yourself and open the attachment in Apple Mail. After importing, check that the event and its alerts appear in Calendar. If the alert is quiet, check Calendar sounds, Silent mode, and Focus settings, then repeat the test.

What does someone receive when I share this guide?

The guide link opens these instructions. Each person enters and keeps their own medication plan on their device. To save a backup of your own plan, open Dosewell apps → Download my plan in the web planner where your medications are saved.
