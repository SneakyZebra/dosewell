Dosewell mobile apps

Android

Dosewell rings for your dose and advance warnings, even when the app is closed. Install the app, then choose Reminders → Enable Dosewell alarms. Allow notifications and exact alarms when Android asks.

Download Dosewell for Android

Version 1.3 includes the free DDInter search for 160,235 published drug pairs. Dose alarms and advance warnings use a gentle chime with vibration. If Dosewell is already installed, open the download and choose Update.

iPhone

Open Dosewell in Safari to use your medication planner without an account or app download. Add a Home Screen icon if you like, and use Apple Calendar for reminders when the screen is locked.

Bring your medications with you

Open this page in the browser where your plan is saved. Download your plan. In the iPhone web planner, open Transfer a saved plan to import it. In the native app, choose Reminders → Import a saved plan. This file includes private medication information and dose history.

After testing the new app alarm, review any older Clock alarms or calendar events you set for the same doses.
