Dosewell mobile apps

Android

Dosewell Reminders 2.1 plays a gentle chime and vibrates for doses and advance warnings. The chime uses Android alarm volume when the ringer is on silent.

Download Dosewell Reminders for Android

Tap a medication name to see patient information from MedlinePlus. If you already use Dosewell Reminders 2.0, open the installer and choose Update to keep your plan. For a first installation, choose Install.

In the new app, open Reminders → Enable Dosewell alarms. Allow notifications and exact alarms. Raise your alarm volume, select Test in 15 seconds, and lock your screen. If you use Do Not Disturb, allow alarms in that mode.

Moving from Dosewell 1.5.1

The two apps can stay on the same phone. In Dosewell Reminders, import a saved Dosewell plan under Reminders or enter your medications from the original app. Check each dose, time and advance warning. When the test chimes and your new schedule is ready, select Reminders → Turn off alarms in the original Dosewell app. Keep it installed to view your old dose history.

Open Dosewell in Safari to use your medication planner without an account or app download. Add a Home Screen icon if you like, and use Apple Calendar for reminders when the screen is locked.

Bring your medications with you

Open this page in the browser where your plan is saved. Download your plan. In the iPhone web planner, open Transfer a saved plan to import it. In the native app, choose Reminders → Import a saved plan. This file includes private medication information and dose history.

After testing the new app alarm, review any older Clock alarms or calendar events you set for the same doses.
