Dosewell mobile apps

Android

Dosewell rings for your dose and advance warnings, even when the app is closed. Install the app, then choose Reminders → Enable Dosewell alarms. Allow notifications and exact alarms when Android asks.

Download Dosewell for Android

iPhone

The iPhone version uses Apple’s AlarmKit on iOS 26 or later. Public installation is pending Apple signing and distribution. The Xcode project is available for a Mac developer to build and sign.

Download iPhone Xcode project

Bring your medications with you

Open this page in the browser where your plan is saved. Download your plan, then open Reminders in the new app and choose Import a saved plan. This file includes private medication information and dose history.

After testing the new app alarm, review any older Clock alarms or calendar events you set for the same doses.
